## About Me

I am a direct Ph.D. candidate in Control Science and Engineering at the School of Frontier Interdisciplinary Science, University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS). I am jointly trained with the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS).

My work focuses on reliable visual inspection systems that can generalize across domains and reduce the cost of high-quality annotation.

## Research Interests

- Industrial anomaly detection and robust visual inspection
- Computer vision and object detection
- Domain adaptation and generalization
- Active learning and uncertainty-aware learning

## Selected Projects

### Active Learning for Domain-Adaptive Object Detection

**Undergraduate Thesis | Dec 2024 - Jun 2025**

- Designed a domain-adaptive object detection framework combining a Probabilistic Teacher model with UCCL active learning.
- Developed uncertainty scoring across classification and localization to improve pseudo-label generation and high-value sample selection.
- Improved mAP over the baseline by **1.5%**, **3.2%**, **2.4%**, and **4.5%** on C2F, C2B, S2C, and K2C tasks.
- Outperformed mainstream unsupervised domain-adaptation methods with only a **5% annotation budget**.

### LLM-Based Corporate Credit Rating Report Generation

**China Collegiate Computer Design Competition | Mar 2024 - May 2024**

- Built an automated credit-rating report generation workflow using LLMs, data processing, and task-specific prompt optimization.
- Designed the rating-model framework and data-processing modules; evaluated it with data from 200 companies.
- Achieved **95% prediction accuracy** on the evaluation set.

### Customer Repurchase Frequency Prediction

**Sichuan Financial Technology Modeling Competition | Nov 2023 - Dec 2023**

- Led data preprocessing, feature selection, and model optimization using SMOTE, SelectKBest, logistic regression, random forests, and SVMs.
- Reached the **top 8%** among 1,215 participants.
